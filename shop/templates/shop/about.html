The Vision Behind Soloma

"Soloma exists for a woman on the go — the one moving between a boardroom, a school pickup, dinner with friends, who needs clothes that move as freely as she does. Real pockets. Fabric that doesn't wrinkle in a bag. Nothing that requires a change of shoes."
